Part Picker User Guide

Part Picker is designed for your phone at the shelves. It turns the part number from a shelf barcode into a fresh, easy-to-scan barcode on your phone. You then scan that phone screen at the booking-out computer. It does not change stock levels or automatically send anything to the booking-out system.

1. At the shelf: capture the part number

  1. Tap Scan Shelf Barcode near the top of the screen. This is the single camera-scan button.
  2. Allow camera access if prompted, then point the phone camera at the barcode on the shelf/part.
  3. Check the Part Number field. If the shelf barcode cannot be read, type the part number manually instead.
  4. Optionally add a shelf location or description, and set Qty to the number of times that part needs to be booked out.
  5. If Automatically add scanned parts is enabled, a successful scan is added to the list automatically. Otherwise tap Add to List. Repeat for each part you need to collate.

2. Check and save your part set

  • Review the Active Scan List and correct any part number, quantity or description before using the barcodes.
  • When the list is ready, tap Generate Barcodes. This opens the booking-out view on your phone.
  • Tap Save This Part Set for a reusable template, or Save As Job Card to save it under a job/bus number. Saved sets can be loaded again later.
  • Use Find a part or the saved-set filter to find previously used parts and templates. Favourite frequently used sets, or use Last Used Set for the most recent one.

3. At the booking-out computer: scan the phone

  1. Open the correct saved job card/template, or open the active list's generated barcodes.
  2. In the barcode viewer, keep Show each barcode once per required quantity enabled if you need to scan each unit separately. Turn it off if one scan per part number is what your booking-out process expects.
  3. Hold your phone screen steady and scan the displayed barcode with the computer's barcode scanner. The barcode encodes the part number, not the description or shelf location. The app can keep the screen awake while this view is open when your phone/browser supports it.
  4. Use Prev and Next to move through the list. Confirm the booking-out system accepted each scan before moving on.

4. Helpful reminders

  • Generated barcodes are a convenient substitute for manually typing the part number; they do not automatically transfer the list to the computer.
  • Your saved sets and history are stored in this browser on this phone. Use Backup data periodically, especially before changing phones or clearing browser data.
  • Open Settings to control automatic scan-to-list, sound, vibration and the barcode screen wake-lock behaviour.
  • For printed labels, use Print Barcode Labels on a saved template when a physical set is useful. For normal jobs, scan the barcodes displayed on the phone.
