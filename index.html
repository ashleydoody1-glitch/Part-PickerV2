Part Picker User Guide

Welcome to Part Picker, your mobile-first inventory and job card companion. This guide explains how to scan, search, manage job cards, load templates, and zap barcodes at your computer terminal.

1. Getting Started & Searching

  • Database Search: Tap Search Database at the top to instantly search your entire history, saved jobs, and templates for terms like "thermostat" or "filter".
  • Camera Scanning: Tap Scan, point your camera at any 1D barcode or orange shelf label.

2. Adding Parts & Templates

  • Templates to Job Cards: Under My Template Groups, tap Load to List to instantly load a saved template into your active scan list, then tap **Save to Job Card**.
  • Locations: Each part displays its bin location (e.g. 3A2) so you can easily locate it in the store.

3. Active List & Terminal Integration

  • Screen Barcodes: Open any saved Job Card or Template, tap Screen Barcodes, and zap each large barcode directly off your phone screen using your wireless Zebra scanner.
